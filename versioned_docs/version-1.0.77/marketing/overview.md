# Marketing & Engagement Overview

The Marketing module is the home for outbound and patient-facing content: SEO, the information centre, marketing camps and activities, feedback and grievance forms, and a **Configurables** screen that controls how the patient app, doctor app and queue TV screens look and behave.

## What lives under Marketing

From the Marketing sidebar you can reach:

- **SEO** — search engine settings.
- **Info Center** — information centre content.
- **Feedback Forms** — the templates used to collect patient feedback.
- **Marketing Camp** and **Marketing Activities** — outreach campaigns and the activities within them.
- **Marketing Camp Form** — the form used to capture camp data.
- **Grievances Management**, **Grievance Reports** and **Grievance Forms** — patient grievance handling.
