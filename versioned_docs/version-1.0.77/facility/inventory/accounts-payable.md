## Attaching the invoice document

Voucher and invoice entries carry an **upload** action so the supplier's invoice can be attached to the record it belongs to, rather than being filed separately.

- The upload button sits on the invoice row in both voucher entry and payment entry.
- Where no document has been attached yet, the row indicates that the invoice is **not uploaded**, so gaps are visible at a glance.
- Once uploaded, the document can be opened from the same place by anyone reviewing or approving the payment.

:::tip
Attach the invoice at entry rather than at payment. The person approving the payment can then verify it against the document without having to ask for it.
:::
