# The "Our Doctors" Board

A Queue TV screen with nothing in its queue used to sit empty. Instead, it now shows an **Our Doctors** board — a directory of the doctors at that site.

## At a glance

```steps
fa-tv | Queue is empty | Nobody is currently being called.
fa-users | Our Doctors appears | The screen shows the site's doctors instead.
fa-id-card | A card per doctor | Photo, name, designation and room.
fa-refresh | Pages turn automatically | Long lists cycle on their own.
fa-bell | Queue resumes | The board steps aside when a token is called.
```

## What the board shows

Every doctor belonging to the screen's assigning authority is shown as a card carrying:

- Their **photo**. Where no photo is on file, a default illustration is used, matched to the doctor's recorded gender.
- Their **name**.
- Their **designation**, falling back to their speciality and then their department.
- Their **room**.

## Long doctor lists

Where there are more doctors than fit on one screen, the board **paginates** and advances through the pages automatically, so every doctor is shown in turn without anyone touching the display.

## When the board appears

The board fills the screen only while the queue is empty — no token in progress and none waiting. As soon as a patient is called, the screen returns to the queue display.

:::tip
This turns dead screen time into wayfinding. Patients waiting in a corridor can see which doctors are in and which room to go to, without asking at the desk.
:::
