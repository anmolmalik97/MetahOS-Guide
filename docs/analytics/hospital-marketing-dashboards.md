# Hospital Marketing Dashboards

The **Hospital Marketing** department groups together the marketing- and patient-growth analytics in MetahOS. Open it from the AI Dashboard landing page (the **Hospital Marketing** card) or directly at `/aidashboard/hospital-marketing`.

The Hospital Marketing landing page lists eight dashboards. Each one opens its own analytics view with a date-range filter and the currency switcher described in the [AI Dashboards Overview](./ai-dashboards-overview.md).

## Dashboards in this department

| Dashboard | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Patient Acquisition & Channel Attribution** | New patients, leads, scheduled vs. completed consultations, lead sources, lead-status funnel, top departments and acquisition-channel attribution. Powered by live data. |
| **Brand Sentiment & NPS** | Grievances, PRM feedback (complaints / queries / suggestions), feedback links sent, resolution rates and sentiment trends. Powered by live data. |
| **Campaign Performance & ROI Report** | Appointments and revenue this month, channel mix, a 12-month report table and a leads → appointments → completed conversion funnel. |
| **Patient Retention & Re-engagement** | Returning vs. lapsed patients, re-engagement trends and specialty breakdowns. |
| **Health Camp & Community Outreach Impact Report** | Screenings, issues raised and outreach impact sourced from the Camps module. |
| **Digital Footprint & SEO Performance** | Online presence and search-performance indicators. |
| **Doctor Referral Network Analysis** | Inbound/outbound referral volumes, top referrers and referral trends by specialty. |
| **Package & Service Bundle Performance** | Health-package and service-bundle sales, utilisation and revenue. |

The two dashboards built on live MetahOS data — **Patient Acquisition** and **Brand Sentiment & NPS** — are documented in detail on the following pages:

- [Brand Sentiment & NPS Dashboard](./brand-sentiment-nps-dashboard.md)

## Common controls

### Date range

Most Hospital Marketing dashboards include a **date-range filter**. When no range is selected the dashboards default to the **last 12 months** for trend charts and the **current month** for summary cards.

### Currency

Use the header currency switcher to toggle every monetary value between **INR (₹)** and **USD ($)**.

### Authority scope

Where a dashboard is authority-aware, the figures are automatically scoped to the assigning authority (site / facility) you have access to. Users with all-authority access see organisation-wide numbers.
