## Doctor app home module configurables

The **Home Module Visibility** card lets you choose which modules a doctor sees on the app home screen. Tick a module to show it; untick to hide it. Checked modules are visible on the doctor app home screen.

The configurable home modules are: Consultation Worklist, Consolidated Bills, Schedule, Patient Management, and Dashboard. The visibility map is saved under the home modules configuration.

:::tip
Hide modules a particular deployment does not use so doctors land on a focused home screen. For example, a site that does not bill through the app can hide **Consolidated Bills**.
:::

## Onboarding slides and disclaimers

Alongside home module visibility, the doctor app supports:

- **Onboarding slides** — the first-run walkthrough, saved under the onboarding slides configuration. Each slide carries a title, text and an image.
- **Disclaimers** — saved under the disclaimers configuration.
